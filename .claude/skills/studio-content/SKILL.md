# Fiysam Studio Content Skill

## Positioning

Fiysam Studio is a product/software studio that builds software for clients, builds products for itself, experiments, ships, learns, scales what works, and stops what does not.

It should not be presented as a conventional software agency.

## Voice

Use direct language, concise sentences, confidence without hype, practical product language, specific claims, and intelligent restraint.

Avoid corporate filler, exaggerated promises, vague innovation language, unnecessary jargon, and generic agency copy.

## Truthfulness

Fictional content may be used as explicitly labeled concept/demo content during development. Never present fictional projects, clients, testimonials, metrics, outcomes, team members, or business history as verified facts.

If information is unavailable, omit it or clearly frame it as an idea, capability, experiment, or future direction.

## Product storytelling

The site should eventually support:

- Studio ventures
- Client engagements
- Experiments
- Products
- Case studies

When real work becomes available, prefer evidence-driven storytelling:

1. Problem
2. Opportunity
3. Approach
4. What was built
5. Outcome
6. What was learned

Do not manufacture outcomes.

## Current product state

There are no verified Studio-owned launched products to showcase yet.

Fictional projects may appear only as explicitly labeled Studio Concept or demo content during development; never present them as verified client work, launched products, or evidence of business history.

Structure the information architecture so future real work can be added naturally.

## Information architecture

Potential future structures may include `/work`, `/work/[slug]`, product/project detail pages, and experiments or studio-log content.

Do not implement these routes unless the current task calls for them.

## Calls to action

Prefer concrete CTAs such as:

- Start a project
- Build with us
- Tell us what you're building
- Work with Studio
- Explore the work

Avoid vague CTA language such as:

- Transform your business
- Unlock innovation
- Discover the future

unless the context makes it specific.

## Fiysam relationship

The Studio may reference its relationship to Fiysam where useful, but should maintain its own voice and product-focused positioning.
