# Fiysam Studio Design Skill

Create interfaces that feel like a serious product studio, not a generic software agency and not a copy of Fiysam's corporate website.

## Brand balance

Use approximately:

- 30–40% Fiysam brand DNA
- 60–70% Studio-specific expression

Retain the dark/obsidian foundation, amber/gold accent, confident typography, technical precision, strong contrast, and Nigerian/West African context.

Evolve them through editorial composition, product-oriented layouts, asymmetric structure, generous whitespace, interface/product artifacts, technical annotations, and controlled motion.

## Visual principles

1. Establish hierarchy before decoration.
2. Use whitespace intentionally.
3. Avoid making every section a card grid.
4. Use amber selectively for emphasis, interaction, status, or important details.
5. Prefer real product/interface imagery over generic stock imagery.
6. Use motion to clarify hierarchy or interaction.
7. Preserve readability and accessibility at every breakpoint.
8. Make experimental layouts feel intentional and structured.

## Experimental does not mean

- neon everywhere
- random gradients
- excessive glassmorphism
- purposeless floating 3D objects
- AI-startup clichés
- excessive animation
- visual noise

## Imagery

Prefer real product screenshots, prototypes, interfaces, diagrams, technical drawings, authentic project artifacts, and carefully art-directed photography when appropriate.

Do not fabricate evidence of products or client work.

## Responsive design

Design mobile as a first-class composition, not a collapsed desktop layout.

Check typography, spacing, navigation, image cropping, section ordering, interaction targets, overflow, and motion behavior.

## Accessibility

Maintain sufficient contrast, semantic structure, keyboard accessibility, visible focus states, meaningful alt text, reduced-motion considerations, and accessible labels for icon-only controls.
